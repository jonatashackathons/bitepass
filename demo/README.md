## 🎬 Slow-Motion Demonstration Video

The demo video is recorded in slow-motion pacing with clear on-screen chyrons providing plain-language human business explanations at every stage, accompanied by an upbeat, warm lo-fi background soundtrack:

1. **Step 01/05 · Proactive AI:** AI spots a 35-minute calendar gap and serves lunch before hunger strikes. No 50-item menus.
2. **Step 02/05 · 1-Tap Tweak:** Swap sides in 3 seconds with real-time recalculation and zero form-filling.
3. **Step 03/05 · PayPal Escrow:** $0 charged upfront; funds protected in escrow while the kitchen starts immediate fresh assembly.
4. **Step 04/05 · Doorstep Pass:** Auto-settles when crossing the 50-meter threshold. FASTPASS unlocks Cubby #D for 0-second grab-and-go.
5. **Step 05/05 · Kitchen Ops:** AG Grid provides live kitchen operations telemetry, bay capacity control, and zero cashier overhead.
