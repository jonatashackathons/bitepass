## 📝 3. Comprehensive Video Description (For Loom / YouTube / YC Portal)

```markdown
BitePass is the world's first autonomous proactive micro-commerce agent for workplace dining. 

Every single workday, over 50 million office professionals lose 25 minutes of their lunch break to the "food order tax"—scrolling through 60-item menus, waiting in noisy lines, and typing credit card numbers. Delivery apps don't fix this: they tack on $10+ in fees and deliver soggy, lukewarm sandwiches 20 minutes late.

BitePass solves this with "The Culinary Fast Pass":
1. Ambient AI Detection: Powered by Gemini 4 Argon, BitePass monitors calendar gaps, models kitchen prep time at artisan venues downstairs, and serves a tailored, 1-tap meal pass right before hunger strikes.
2. 2-Phase PayPal Escrow: Tap once to hold funds in escrow ($0 charged upfront). The kitchen receives a guaranteed hold and preps fresh food while you walk downstairs.
3. Doorstep Auto-Capture (<50m): The exact millisecond your phone crosses the 50-meter cafe threshold, BitePass automatically settles payment via PayPal and unlocks your designated heated pickup cubby.

Zero lines. Zero menus. Zero waiting. Fresh hot food ready the moment you walk through the door.
```
